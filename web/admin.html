Store admin

Sign in to manage prices and print QR tags.

Prices

Shoppers see the Shown price: your store price, or ~5% below the cheapest online price if that is lower.

Leave a competitor price empty if that store doesn't sell the product. For bulk edits, download the Excel file, change the price columns and upload it back. Rows are matched by QR code, and nothing is saved if any row has an error.

QR tags

Print these. The Master QR goes at the entrance: it opens the store and sets the shopper's location to the Entrance. Each shelf tag goes on its product: it opens that product's page with today's price and sets the shopper's location to that aisle. Tags show no prices, so they never go out of date.

Tags contain this address. Open this admin page from the same network (or tunnel) your shoppers will use, and make the tags only after you are connected. If the network changes, the tags must be made again.

AI content for the product page

Drafts are written by the local Llama model from the product data. Review and edit them, then save. Nothing is published until you click Save.
