# OmniTag Mall store information (demo content)

This document describes the policies of the fictional OmniTag Mall used in this project. It is sample data, not a real store's terms.

## Returns and exchanges
Items can be returned or exchanged within 10 days of purchase with the original bill and tags intact. Clothing and footwear must be unworn and unwashed. Electronics such as phones and earbuds can be exchanged within 10 days only if the seal is unbroken or the item is found defective on arrival. Earbuds and other in-ear audio products are not returnable once opened for hygiene reasons, but defective pieces are replaced. Refunds go back to the original payment method in 5 to 7 working days. Items bought on a special offer or clearance can be exchanged but not refunded.

## Warranty and repairs
Electronics carry the manufacturer's warranty, usually 1 year for phones and earbuds. The mall helps you register the warranty at the service desk on the ground floor. Physical damage, water damage and unauthorised repairs are not covered. For a defective phone in the first 10 days, the mall will arrange a replacement directly; after that, the brand's authorised service centre handles repairs. Clothing and footwear have a 30 day check for stitching and sole defects.

## Price match promise
The mall prices products about 5 percent below the lowest price it finds on Amazon, Flipkart and Croma, but never above the store's own listed price. Online prices are checked by the store staff and may change daily. If you find a lower price from one of these three online stores for the same model, show the page to the service desk and they will review it. Prices on the tag and the product page are the final prices, including taxes. Staff cannot negotiate additional discounts on individual items.

## Payment options and EMI
We accept cash, UPI, debit cards and credit cards. No-cost EMI for 3 or 6 months is available on purchases above 10000 rupees with selected credit cards. Smaller purchases can use UPI or card with no extra charge. Bills are issued digitally by SMS or email, and a printed copy is available on request.

## Offers and loyalty
Members of the OmniTag Rewards programme earn 1 reward point for every 100 rupees spent, and 100 points are worth 50 rupees on a future purchase. Membership is free at the service desk with a phone number. Festival sales run during Diwali, Eid, Christmas and the end of season sale; the discounts shown on tags already include these offers. Offers cannot be combined with each other.

## Store timings and location
The mall is open from 10 AM to 9 PM every day, including weekends and most public holidays. Aisles 1 and 2 hold mobiles and audio, Aisles 3 and 4 hold men's and women's apparel, and Aisles 5 and 6 hold footwear. The service desk, billing counters and trial rooms are near the entrance. Parking is available in the basement and is free for the first 2 hours with a bill from the mall.

## Delivery and gifting
Large items can be delivered to your home within the city for a flat fee of 99 rupees, free on bills above 5000 rupees. Delivery usually takes 2 to 3 days. Gift wrapping is available at the service desk for 30 rupees. Gift receipts hide the price and allow exchange within 10 days.

## Help and accessibility
Staff in blue jackets can help you find any product, and the service desk offers wheelchairs, baby strollers and a charging point for phones. The store has lifts and wide aisles for accessibility. Shoppers can ask the OmniTag assistant on their phone for directions to any aisle.
